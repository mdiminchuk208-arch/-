## Exclusions and sensitivity

Deribit scores 75 and Hyperliquid 74 before hard exclusions, above BloFin. Deribit's dedicated subaccount does not establish required position-level isolated margin/leverage equivalence. Hyperliquid's agent-wallet authority does not prove separation from all external asset movement. Neither is promoted on low fees alone. OKX, Gate and MEXC fail ordinary API-fee proof; MEXC also lacks confirmed safe key scope. Binance returns HTTP451 and Bybit HTTP403 from both cloud and ordinary CI, without evasion. Coinbase International's BTC/ETH products are PAUSED, current candles/books are unavailable, and its fee route is restricted. KuCoin, Gate, Hyperliquid and MEXC lack the accepted two-year native-M15 sentinel/archive prerequisite for primary research. Exact reasons are retained in `REJECTED_VENUES.json` and the ranked hard-gate arrays.

Bitget research and BloFin execution remain primary under both preregistered ±5-point execution-cost weight shifts and all 76 leave-one-snapshot-out cases. Conservative unknown-depth treatment and promotion exclusion do not change those primaries. **Secondary execution is not fully robust:** removing the single insufficient KuCoin ETH CI book switches Kraken to KuCoin in 1 of 76 cases. This sensitivity is disclosed, not used to discard the book or choose a more favorable winner. Kraken and KuCoin are conditional reserve candidates, neither slippage-qualified by the complete present evidence grid.
