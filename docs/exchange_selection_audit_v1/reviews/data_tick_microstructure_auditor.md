## Source and calculation checks

- Local indexed raw paths for all 12 venues resolved and matched their SHA-256 where listed; the final `SOURCE_HASH_VERIFICATION.json` reports **503** raw/receipt verifications across local and CI capture with status PASS. `FROZEN_INPUT_VERIFICATION.json` reports 606 parent manifest hashes verified without parsing financial content. Hash checks establish integrity of retained evidence, not correctness of venue claims. Source IDs resolve through each venue `SOURCE_INDEX.json` or CI receipt to official URLs, UTC times, status, and hashes.
- Exact old rows were inspected in retained JSON bodies, including timestamp `1728777600000` ms or ISO `2024-10-13T00:00:00Z`. OKX's `after` probes yielded old rows; its `before` probes returned current rows and must not be cited as old evidence. Bitget returned one old row per symbol; BloFin ten by direct `candles`; Kraken 97 in an inclusive one-day window; Deribit and Coinbase nine each. None establishes continuity between old and current samples.
- For normalized books, use `Decimal` arithmetic and require positive prices/quantities, bid < ask, correct symbol/product, and receipt/exchange timestamps. Sort asks ascending for BUY and bids descending for SELL before walking levels. Partial final levels must fill by remaining **notional**, and insufficient cumulative depth must return an unfilled result, not a favorable extrapolation. Compute impact from contemporaneous mid and include spread crossing. Deduplicate prices only if exact aggregation semantics are documented.
- Do not score crossed, empty, IP-blocked, or unit-ambiguous books as valid liquidity. Enforce actual exchange/receipt timestamp identity and completeness; do not invent a post-observation freshness threshold. The combined local and CI pool uses all valid retained observations rather than choosing the most favorable. Near-simultaneous CI captures supplement older local samples; duplicated exchange timestamp plus identical raw hash, if encountered, should count once. The same UTC instant across venues is not guaranteed, so relative price levels are not an arbitrage signal.
- Current metadata is usable to timestamp a prospective collector. Historical tick/filter epochs require dated retained source snapshots or authoritative change records for the applicable symbol and effective time. Hyperliquid price validation must implement its significant-digit rule as a function of price and `szDecimals`; mapping it to a constant BTC/ETH tick would be wrong even today.
