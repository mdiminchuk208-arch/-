## Opinion at evidence stage

**Conditional architecture selection is supportable for venues whose public ordinary fee schedule and protective API controls are established; live activation is not.** Public documentation verifies plausible bot controls at several venues, but this is architecture evidence, not an account-level activation test. Unknown actual account tier and legal eligibility should be recorded as future activation checks, **not** converted into an automatic hard failure for every venue with a credible current ordinary public fee schedule. Conversely, an example fee, Spot table, promotion, or public instrument field with unresolved API/account applicability must not be promoted into a known base API rate. The prerequisite is a defensible current ordinary API trading-class rate, not the impossible claim that the eventual private account was already tested in this public-only gate.

At equal entry/exit notionals, the verified *fee-only* taker/taker round trips are Deribit 7 bps, Hyperliquid 9 bps, Kraken 10 bps, Bybit 11 bps, and Bitget/BloFin/KuCoin 12 bps. One basis point is 0.01% of notional; 7 bps costs $0.70/$3.50/$7/$17.50/$35 at $1k/$5k/$10k/$25k/$50k. Multiply by 9/7, 10/7, 11/7 or 12/7 for the other known schedules. Maker/taker equals maker + taker (also the 50/50 side-mix expectation), and maker/maker equals 2 × maker. Those are fee components only: the preregistered primary **all-in** cost uses taker/taker fees plus both mid-referenced book impacts. Maker fill probability, adverse selection and queue priority are unproven; maker scenarios must not drive the primary rank. Exit notional can differ from entry notional in actual fills.

Funding must stay in its own signed ledger, with interval, formula/caps, history and platform surcharge evidence separately scored. Hyperliquid explicitly says funding is peer-to-peer with no fee and pays hourly using one eighth of an eight-hour computed rate (`hl_docs_funding`). Deribit documents continuously accrued USDC perpetual funding, an eight-hour quoted rate, daily settlement, positive-rate long payer and no platform funding fee (`funding_spec`, `docs_funding_md`). OKX live BTC/ETH intervals were eight hours but can change and only three months of public history are documented (`funding_btc`, `funding_eth`, `docs_main`). Bitget, BloFin, Gate, KuCoin and MEXC currently show eight-hour intervals; the retrieved sources do not prove all of their payer/payee sign rules, formula epochs or absence of surcharge. Kraken PF observed hourly historical rows, while an older example differs. Coinbase's last funding record is stale with paused products. Never convert a single funding print into venue preference or add its magnitude to taker commission without a defined position horizon and direction. A `null` surcharge means **unverified**, not zero.

## Control requirements for a later adapter and activation gate

All protective checks must use the actual product and account mode. A documented field is evidence of a possible API architecture, not evidence that the target account has been configured. Before any live activation, use a no-withdrawal key or proved equivalent scoped signer, restrict IP where supported, verify order/position query reconciliation and client-ID retry semantics, and exercise isolated margin, leverage, reduce-only close and exchange-side stop/TP behavior in a demo environment. A client ID that is unique only among currently open orders is not a durable idempotency guarantee. No live order or private call belongs in this exchange-selection audit.

The Hyperliquid signing model needs its own security proof. The retained `hl_docs_wallet` says agents can sign on behalf of master/subaccounts and warns about nonce pruning/replay after deregistration. The retained `hl_docs_exchange` documents `agentSendAsset` as agent-signed with destination equal to source, and also separately documents `withdraw3`, `spotSend` and other transfer actions. The raw text does not explicitly prove whether an agent signature can authorize each external movement action. Do not treat the same-address condition for one action as a blanket withdrawal prohibition. The key-scope hard gate remains unresolved until authoritative action/signature restrictions or a bounded nonfunded test establish it.

## Final scoring cross-check

The generated cost calculation follows the frozen primary rule: two taker fees plus two times the equal-symbol/size/side mean mid-referenced impact. BloFin's 12.12725964327464574 bps all-in estimate is based on retained local and ordinary hosted-CI books and maps to execution score 69 with its hard gates true. Deribit (7.025726406117107471 bps) and Hyperliquid (9.280706990684755144 bps) have higher raw scores, 75 and 74, but fail the isolated-control and agent-signer safety hard gates respectively. No fee rebate or promotion enters this score. OKX and Gate have usable books but unknown base API fee, so cost is null under preregistration. A `null` cost is not zero cost.

The final `LIQUIDITY_SPREAD_SLIPPAGE.json` pools all retained valid local and ordinary hosted-CI samples under the preregistered sample rule. Bitget and Kraken have enough observed book rows, but the source evidence does not prove their book size unit, so `book_units_confirmed=false` makes their liquidity and cost criteria zero. KuCoin's units are proved and its execution hard gates now pass, yet the CI ETH book cannot fill the $50,000 BUY quote budget; `complete_sizes_and_two_samples=false` also makes both criteria zero. Thus Kraken and KuCoin tie at execution score 29, and the frozen lexicographic tie rule selects Kraken as secondary; Bitget is 28. These zero scores express missing executable comparison evidence, not a claim of poor actual liquidity. `LOT_GRID_VWAP.json` separately checks current lot-valid quantities and does not alter the frozen continuous quote-budget scoring model.

The final roles in `VENUE_ROLE_DECISIONS.json` are research Bitget (82, tied with OKX 82 and selected by the lexicographic rule), primary future execution BloFin (69), secondary future execution Kraken (29 tie), and reference benchmark OKX. These are architecture roles only. Account-specific fees, account mode, jurisdiction and full protective-order behavior remain for a later compatibility/activation gate; no 365-day clock, paper or live trading follows from this review.
