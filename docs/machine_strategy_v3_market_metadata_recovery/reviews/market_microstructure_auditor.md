# Market microstructure audit — same agent as historical data engineer

I performed this role after the historical-data review because the parent could not start the separately configured agent. This is a distinct audit scope by **the same agent**, not an independent second reviewer. I read metadata contracts, official documentation examples and pinned M15 bars only; no financial outcomes or historical runner execution.

The semantic field needed for exchange order-price executability is Bybit `priceFilter.tickSize` for the exact `category=linear`, `contractType=LinearPerpetual`, `symbol=BTCUSDT` or `ETHUSDT`, quote/settle USDT instrument. The retained official instrument documentation (`docs/original_v1_1_metadata_recovery_v2/provenance/OFFICIAL_INSTRUMENT_pre_2023.mdx`, lines 46–55) calls `tickSize` “The minimal step to increase/reduce order price”; `priceScale` is described separately as price scale. An example's `priceScale`, chart display decimal count, and kline OHLC precision do not establish executable order-price increments or their active dates. The documentation examples themselves are not production instrument snapshots, as `OFFICIAL_DOCUMENT_EXAMPLE_ASSESSMENT.json` records. Other contract categories, such as spot, cannot be substituted even when their symbol text matches.

The ETH and BTC Bybit notices state before/after tick changes, but they lack complete implemented-filter and unchanged-interval proof. The archived ETH page was captured after its stated effective date; the BTC retained page was retrieved in 2026. Neither supports a complete 2022–2025 executable grid. The exact-Decimal consistency audit in `reviews/historical_data_engineer.md` found one BTC cell off the claimed `0.5` pre-change grid at `2023-02-10T07:45Z` and six ETH cells across four bars off `0.05` at `2023-01-17T07:00Z`–`07:45Z`. Both changes are claimed for `08:00Z`. Those prices appear identically in the public mirror's raw-source aliases. The mismatch may indicate notice chronology, source authenticity, or instrument semantics problems; M15 candles cannot resolve which. It would be unsound to backdate the finer tick using the first finer OHLC or round the candles to fit the notice.

The frozen core's `observe_m15_bar` checks all OHLC and entry/stop/target against the active `Decimal` tick and waits on off-grid values (`scripts/machine_strategy_v3_contracts.py`, lines 709–743). It waits for unresolved intrabar ordering, and an OHLC touch is modeled as a full limit fill (`TOUCH_IMPLIES_FULL_LIMIT_FILL_AT_E`, lines 744–790). Even a verified price grid would not by itself establish queue priority, available depth, partial fills, spread at event time, or precise event ordering from M15. These are execution-model limits; they do not justify changing strategy rules in this metadata gate.

**Finding:** price-grid authenticity remains UNVERIFIED for both full frozen intervals. Preserve financial STOP. Require immutable, dated production `priceFilter.tickSize` evidence for the exact linear perpetual and independently assess candle conflicts before any exchange-realistic label.
