# Strategy explorer: frozen v3 implementation map

Scope: nonfinancial, read-only comparison of the frozen v3 design with reusable predecessor code and the new readiness adapter. This review does not run historical execution, inspect historical outcomes, or propose a subgroup trading rule.

## Inheritance and overrides

`docs/machine_strategy_v3_design/RULE_PROVENANCE.json` lists 29 inherited M2 rules: 19 reusable without override and 10 overridden. The overridden IDs are `M2_TF`, `M2_CONTEXT`, `M2_HTF`, `M2_FIRST_TEST`, `M2_IDENTITIES`, `M2_ENTRY`, `M2_STOP`, `M2_FTA`, `M2_RANGE_EXIT`, and `M2_INTRABAR`; implementation must use the corresponding frozen v3 contracts rather than importing old v2 behavior. Reusable raw definitions are `M2_SWING`, `M2_BOS`, `M2_CONF`, `M2_IMPULSE`, `M2_PD_OTE`, `M2_LIQ_SCOPE`, `M2_LIQ_ROLES`, `M2_FVG`, `M2_OB`, `M2_SFP`, `M2_STB`, `M2_BREAKER`, `M2_RANGE`, `M2_SMOOTH`, `M2_POI_PRIORITY`, `M2_BRANCH_PRIORITY`, `M2_EXPIRY`, `M2_SOURCE_BOUNDARY`, and `M2_SFP_BODY_EXIT`. The rule text is in `docs/machine_strategy_v2_design/MACHINE_RULE_REGISTRY.csv`; the v3 replacements are in `docs/machine_strategy_v3_design/MACHINE_STRATEGY_V3_SPEC.md`, `DIRECTION_AND_REGIME_SPEC.json`, `RISK_NORMALIZATION_SPEC.json`, `ENTRY_TARGET_EXECUTION_SPEC.json`, and `SETUP_FAMILY_SPEC.json`.

`scripts/original_v1_1_objective_geometry_v2.py` `GeometryIndex` is useful for raw strict swings, BOS-origin candidates, FVG, SFP, raid-engulf OB and STB. It precomputes a full series, so an adapter may expose an event only after its `known_at`/`completed_end` and must not access future `by_id` events. `scripts/machine_strategy_v2_core.py` `Frame` provides native BOS/CONF primitives, but its single current context, single pending BOS, first-test handling and mutable range lifecycle are not a drop-in v3 state machine. `raw_pending` can seed family formation; `build_cohort` embeds superseded H4 gating, stop, target and intrabar assumptions and cannot decide v3 admission. The mandatory source path is contiguous M15 primary bars resampled to UTC H1, H4 and D1. Separately normalized H1/H4 files and six `source_raw` aliases cannot be mixed into that path; their boundaries and hashes differ.

## Five frozen formation families

1. **SFP:** H1/H4 wick raids an already known strict swing and body reclaims it. The next native OPEN reclaim is observable only at that native bar CLOSE. Freeze the original pattern extreme and completed pattern-end SMA14 true range. After that reaction, require matching M15 BOS/CONF and a newly born exact BOS-origin OB or FVG wholly inside the original pattern; choose OB before FVG, then latest `born_at` and canonical ID. The original pattern extreme remains the body-exit anchor; the buffered raw boundary is separate.
2. **OB:** An opposing native candle raids a known swing and the next native candle engulfs its full range. Preserve that original candle interval/extreme. After native reaction and matching M15 BOS/CONF, the selected new exact BOS-origin M15 OB/FVG must be entirely inside the original interval.
3. **BREAKER:** The parent native OB must be contextual at birth and known at the break bar OPEN. A completed opposite body CLOSE crosses its full extreme. Preserve the parent-birth-through-break extreme; then require opposite matching M15 BOS/CONF without intervening reverse BOS and a body-zone retest. The inherited definition does not add an unconsumed-parent gate.
4. **STB:** Use the same raw raid-engulf geometry as OB, followed by matching M15 BOS/CONF. Entry is the manipulation body midpoint and stop uses the complete manipulation extreme. STB and OB are distinct sibling candidates.
5. **RANGE:** Native H1/H4 confirmed impulse, later interior opposing correction strict swing, EQ touch or inside CLOSE, first wick deviation beyond a native boundary and inside reclaim CLOSE, later boundary contact and inside CLOSE, then matching M15 BOS/CONF. The native formation range remains its geometry even where the global H4 state is `RANGE`. The generic `GeometryIndex` range record is not this executable formation.

For each reacted family, the chosen confirmation is the latest available `(known_at, id)` with direction match at the H1 decision. Its linked BOS must have `known_at >= reaction_at`; any opposite M15 BOS in `(reaction_at, chosen_CONF.known_at]` rejects the candidate. Formation expiry uses the inherited H1 decision condition `at > formation_at + 16h`. The v3 order window expires before M15 OPEN `>= decision_at + 1h`.

## Causal state, inventory, and identity

At an H1 decision cutoff, advance only completed D1, H4, H1, then M15 facts. Retain current and immediately prior confirmed contextual legs; if opposite confirmations share a `known_at`, v3 direction is `UNCERTAIN`. Native POI first test begins at the first eligible native bar OPEN after origin confirmation, while its context still matches. The first OHLC contact consumes the POI whether or not an order exists: OPEN contact has time bounds `[bar_start, bar_start]`; wick contact has `[bar_start, bar_end]` and is observed at bar end. Synthetic breaker and range POIs begin only after their prerequisites. A pending idea reserves its entry POI at emission and never retries after fill, loss, expiry, or gap invalidation.

The H1 cutoff target inventory must include every known unswept profit-side strict swing on H1/H4/D1 and confirmed contextual unconsumed opposing OB/STB/FVG, with IDs, bounds, and `known_at`; a native RANGE candidate also has its far-boundary cap. Select the nearest raw signed price distance and preserve tied IDs/digest. Apply the inherited internal adverse pool rejection. Missing geometry, context, complete inventory, or source continuity is WAIT/STOP, never an inferred empty inventory. After raw family prices and inventory, the verified tick epoch and frozen executable-plan math determine admission.

Physical sibling priority is BREAKER > STB > SFP > OB > RANGE, then H4 > H1 and canonical setup ID. The physical bucket is `(symbol, formation_at, direction)`; opposite directions have independent buckets. The POI reservation identity is distinct from the physical bucket.

## Frozen interpretation points

`M2_RANGE` says a new interior correction swing creates a new range identity, while v3 says an *active* range is fixed after its EQ reaction and retired permanently on a completed CLOSE outside the boundary. Treat pre-activation candidate evolution and post-activation fixed lifetime separately; do not silently replace an active range. A wick outside does not retire it. A single pending BOS slot can suppress simultaneous opposite confirmations; this must be detectable before v3 `UNCERTAIN` state is assigned. All five family events require explicit birth, eligibility, observation and consumption timestamps. Any rule ambiguity that cannot be resolved by the frozen texts is a STOP/clarification item, not permission to add a new filter.

## Adapter and runner review

The final static mapping was completed by the agent also serving `backtest_reviewer`, after the original `strategy_explorer` produced the source map above. These are role outputs, not four distinct final reviewers.

The adapter releases D1, H4, H1, and M15 completed bars at each H1 cutoff. `HistoricalFrame` inherits scheduled raw events, BOS/CONF, native POI consumption, and augments range retirement and first-test time bounds. It discovers all five native H1/H4 families, then requires an M15 confirmation and causal context/FTA/tick plan. The directional physical bucket and separate POI reservation are applied at order emission. Historical positive executable order coverage remains stopped by absent verified tick epochs; the synthetic fixtures establish the exercised positive paths without historical financial execution.

The frozen `M2_BREAKER` definition places the body-zone retest **after** opposite subsequent M15 BOS+CONF as a resting limit entry contact. Therefore `raw_pending` correctly sets `reaction_at` to the break time; the guarded order lifecycle observes the later body contact. Requiring a native retest before CONF would add a filter. The frozen `M2_FIRST_TEST` definition separately says synthetic Breaker/Range entry POIs become eligible after branch prerequisites and their first future order contact is recorded in execution. Their absence from native `frame.zones` does not establish missing pre-order consumption. Native contextual raw OB/STB/FVG first tests are handled in `Frame.step` and observed at the completed native bar end. Range is distinct: its boundary reaction precedes M15 CONF, so the adapter correctly waits for a later native completed boundary contact before planning.

The runner-level metadata uncertainty issue identified by `backtest_reviewer` was corrected with per-order unresolved state and internal synthetic fixtures. This mapping finds no additional family-sequence or first-test mismatch. Explorer final verdict: **IMPLEMENTATION_REVIEW_PASS** for the causal source-to-adapter mapping, with historical financial execution still stopped by zero verified tick epochs and OOS separately stopped. This verdict shares a final reviewer with `backtest_reviewer`; it does not claim four distinct people independently reviewed the code.
