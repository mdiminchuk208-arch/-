# MACHINE_STRATEGY_v3_OKX_COMPATIBILITY_v1 — pre-outcome execution policy

Status: **preregistered model specification, not READY or a financial result**. This policy changes only the OKX execution and accounting binding. It does not assert historical OKX tick, lot, minimum size, contract-value, mark-price, or account-fee epochs. The 2026-10-12 public instrument snapshot is a scale reference. Every grid in `QUANTIZATION_STRESS_POLICY.json`, including its reference grid, is a synthetic assumption for 2024-10-13 through 2026-10-13, never a `verified=true` source epoch. No ladder member is a guaranteed bound on an unobserved historical exchange rule or fee.

The protected v3 contracts and historical adapter remain byte-for-byte unchanged. Run only a new versioned OKX adapter/runner after this policy is committed and independently frozen. The adapter must consume exactly the sealed Stage-A official BTC-USDT-SWAP and ETH-USDT-SWAP M15 and signed funding inputs, verify their Stage-A hashes and the parent manifest, derive H1/H4/D1 from completed M15 bars, and retain venue `OKX` at every input and output boundary. The existing BYBIT-bound runner and its authorization cannot be repurposed. No order endpoint or account key is involved. PAPER, LIVE and `trade_entry_allowed` remain false.

## Candidate catalog and invariant decisions

The common catalog is constructed once from the protected v3 pre-entry logic and the reference-grid plan, before looking at any exit, funding cash or financial metric. Record every raw formation, pending expiration/rejection, causally eligible proposal, reference-grid planning rejection, sibling, reservation conflict and emitted reference-grid order with stable IDs and reasons. Only emitted reference-grid orders are the frozen *qualification case set*; all are retained, including every later stress-ineligible, unfilled, ambiguous, unknown or EOF case. The diagnostic pre-order records are retained for audit and are never retroactively converted to wins or CLOSED trades. Reference-grid eligibility is a labeled execution-model decision, not source proof.

For proposals at one decision, keep v3 sibling order BREAKER, STB, SFP, OB, RANGE; then H4 before H1; then setup ID. Keep one physical-idea and one entry-POI reservation at emission. The catalog's selected sibling, target inventory and nearest signed forward raw-distance target with canonical-ID tie break are immutable across stress scenarios. No scenario may substitute a sibling, farther target, different POI or new formation, or release a reservation after nonfill/rejection. Scenario-specific entry/stop/target prices, grid geometry/economics eligibility, quantity, fill and exit observations are execution states of that same case. A scenario-dependent rejection must remain linked to its case ID. Preserve the v3 first-test, expiry, adverse-liquidity, score, regime and all other pre-entry signal rules.

## Scenario and observation rule

Evaluate every catalog order under all nine price-grid × quantity-lot scenarios and both base and doubled fee/slippage costs. The reference grid is one of the nine. Apply v3 directional floor/ceil rules: LONG entry floor, SHORT entry ceil; LONG stop strictly below its raw boundary, SHORT stop strictly above; target strictly inside the already selected nearest raw FTA (LONG below, SHORT above). Recheck the original zone, geometry, opposing-zone, target collision, adverse-liquidity and positive modeled target cash margin conditions. These checks may reject the scenario but cannot delete the case. The economic feasibility check uses the frozen base 6 bp fee + 2 bp slippage model in both cost columns so the doubled-cost sensitivity cannot change pre-entry selection. Execute the doubled costs on the same scenario path; do not use them to reselect trades.

Observe exact, unrounded, finite positive native M15 OHLC. Do not require OHLC to lie on any synthetic grid, round bars to that grid, or assert a candle proves the historical tick. Only synthetic order entry/stop/target prices must lie on the scenario grid. Apply the protected `observe_m15_bar` event order and LONG/SHORT comparisons, except replace the historical-epoch proof/observed-OHLC-grid checks with the explicit scenario ID and executable-price-grid checks. Pending expiry precedes a bar whose open is at/after expiration. An open beyond stop or target invalidates before a pending fill. A limit touch fills at its limit, without open-price improvement. Wick-only fill with a stop or target touch, or postfill dual touch, is `WAIT_INTRABAR_ORDER`; neither can be converted to a win, a stop, or a CLOSED case. A single touch is interval timed; an open gap exits at the observed open; native SFP body invalidation uses its protected completed close after wick tests. Missing contiguous M15 data is unresolved. Pending EOF, filled EOF and unknown filled exits retain their distinct statuses; no synthetic liquidation is inserted.

The fixed risk comparator for a case is `U = abs(E_ref-S_ref) + 0.0008*(E_ref+S_ref)` USDT per one base coin, from the reference plan at order emission. `U` is positive and remains the denominator for that case in **every** grid and cost column. It is not recomputed from stress geometry, size, exit or doubled costs. The modeled desired size is one base coin per case. For scenario lot `L` contracts and contract base quantity `V=ctVal*ctMult`, use `N=floor((1/V)/L)*L` contracts, provided `N>=minSz`, `N>0`, and both entry and exit use the same `N`; otherwise the scenario is ineligible. Actual modeled base quantity is `Q=N*V`, and entry/exit notionals are `Q*entry_price` and `Q*exit_price`. Thus lot rounding can reduce cash exposure; it never increases the desired one-coin exposure. This is a case-level comparison and makes no portfolio capital, margin, leverage, queue, partial-fill or liquidity claim.

## Deterministic adverse aggregation

For each cost column, a scenario with a valid CLOSED exit receives signed net cash and `net_R=net_cash/U` using the cash and funding binding. For each case, take the **minimum** net cash (equivalently minimum `net_R`) across *all* valid CLOSED scenarios. Record its scenario ID; ties use the lexicographically smallest ID. Do this independently for base and doubled costs; do not choose one scenario because it improves either column. Different valid stop/target/gap exits or funding exposures can all be compared; they do not by themselves make a CLOSED case unresolved. The same catalog and one-case denominator apply to both. Each chosen closed case enters WR with `net_R>0` as win; `net_R<=0` stays in the denominator. Gross/net PF, expectancy, net P&L, subgroup results, drawdown, loss streak and concentration use the chosen per-case adverse ledger; drawdown sorts by the selected scenario's exit `observed_at` then canonical case ID. The interval of a wick event remains in its record and is never presented as an exact execution timestamp.

If scenarios disagree on order eligibility, fill versus no-fill, or CLOSED versus non-CLOSED, retain all states and mark the case `COMPATIBILITY_DISAGREEMENT`. Preserve its worst valid CLOSED value as an adverse diagnostic if one exists, but **qualification is STOP**; it is not counted as a real CLOSED case or removed from a denominator to raise WR. Any scenario `WAIT_INTRABAR_ORDER`, unresolved data/funding/price/timing, filled unknown exit or filled EOF also forces STOP, even when another scenario closed. If every scenario is unfilled by expiry or pending EOF, the case remains a non-CLOSED catalog case; it cannot count toward the 200 CLOSED or coin/direction sample minimum. If all scenarios yield valid finite CLOSED exits, the worst closed result is eligible for metrics even if the valid exit types or funding exposures differ. A reference-grid order must never disappear merely because a stress scenario is inconvenient. The zero-filled-unknown-exit criterion remains exactly zero. This deliberately strict compatibility gate can fail without implying the strategy lost money.

The frozen Stage-A qualification thresholds apply unchanged, including WR ≥70%, CLOSED ≥200, base PF ≥1.20, net expectancy and net P&L >0, doubled-cost PF ≥1.00 and doubled-cost net P&L >0, drawdown ≤20R, loss streak ≤10, each coin+direction ≥30 CLOSED and net positive, Wilson 95% lower bound ≥0.60, largest quarter/coin profit shares ≤0.50/0.80, zero filled unknown exits and no critical causality or integrity failure. No threshold or scenario can be revised after P&L access in this version.

`known_at` for structural inputs remains the completed-bar timestamp used by v3. The later current-instrument snapshot is a fixed preregistered *model parameter*, not information a historical trader knew and not `SOURCE_RULE`; it must never be fed into a historical structural decision as if then known. Funding rates are realized future settlements and may enter only post-event cash accounting, never signal, target, fill or qualification-case selection. READY requires a committed policy freeze, new runner integrity guard, deterministic replay, causality QA and independent review. READY authorizes only the one-shot historical development validation.
